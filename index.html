Why it’s $149

It started with a conversation over a Christmas card

Ken Wicker spent 30 years in customer service and sales before starting his handyman business in 2010. Many of the Hernando County customers he met in those early years still call him today.

In 2018, one of them told him the worst mistake she ever made was selling her house to move into assisted living. After her husband passed away, she didn’t know what would break next, or what it would cost to fix, and the worry won.

That conversation changed the business. Ken rebuilt it around one idea: everyday repairs at one simple price, so no one has to give up a home they love over a leaky faucet or a filter they can’t reach.

Questions

Good to know before you call

What does the $149 cover?

Up to 2 hours of labor on any service from our list. Materials are not included.

Why do I buy the materials?

It keeps your cost down and makes sure you get exactly the products you want. Just have everything on site when we begin.

What if the job takes more than 2 hours?

Additional time is billed in quarter-hour increments, so you only pay for the time the job actually takes.

What isn’t included in the $149 visit?

Roofing, major electrical, major plumbing, screening and irrigation.

Do you take on bigger projects?

Yes. Kitchens, baths, flooring, painting, fences, pavers and clean-outs are quoted separately. Call or send a request and Ken will take a look.
